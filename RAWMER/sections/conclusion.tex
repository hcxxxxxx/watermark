\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
\texttt{RAWMER} carries payload bits in block-wise relative mel-energy relations
between reliability-selected bin groups: the classical patchwork
principle~\cite{bender1996,yeo2003} moved before the vocoder, with the already-required
clean reference spent on choosing which groups to use. It is scoped to provider-side
claim checking, and synchronization remains the open problem.
